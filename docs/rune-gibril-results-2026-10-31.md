- Do not add Reel totals to account views, sum unique audiences across Reels, or substitute viewers for reach.
- 1 → 1,575 compares original baseline with the current total; +1,511 is the separately scoped rolling-dashboard metric.
- Toni Morrison's lifetime total includes distribution after sharing to the main feed; no invented trial-only total, promotion time or success benchmark.
- Approximately 15–21 October is the documented preparation sequence, not seven full working days or causal proof of virality.
